Before You Visit

Tax document checklist

The exact documents you need depend on your situation. This general checklist can help you get started.

Identity

Photo ID and Social Security numbers or taxpayer ID numbers for everyone listed on the return.

Income

W-2s, 1099s, retirement income statements, unemployment forms and other applicable income records.

Deductions & Credits

Relevant education, childcare, mortgage interest, charitable contribution and other supporting records.

Last Year's Return

Bring a copy of your most recent tax return if available, plus direct-deposit information if desired.
